The Mind Behind the Lab

Welcome. I am a vision scientist and researcher dedicated to breaking down complex perceptual structures into intuitive, interactive experiences. My background sits at the intersection of cognitive neuroscience, visual geometry, and public education.

Science shouldn't live exclusively behind university paywalls or inside sterile labs. By translating heavy datasets into elegant visual and tactile experiences, we invite the next generation of creative thinkers to see the natural laws governing our universe.
